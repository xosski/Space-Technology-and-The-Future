EVAPS-PC — Electrically Driven Plasma Conditioning System
Polarization / Ionization Crystal Chamber

Proof-of-Concept White Paper — Revision 0.1

Abstract

EVAPS-PC is a laboratory-scale plasma-conditioning architecture intended to investigate whether an electrically generated plasma stream can be divided into controlled plasma channels, passed through a dielectric/crystalline conditioning chamber, and subsequently reconverged into a single conditioned plasma flow.

The proposed architecture removes combustion from the VAPS conversion chain and substitutes a controlled electrical plasma source:

Electrical Input→Plasma Generation→Beam Separation→Crystal/Dielectric Conditioning→Re-convergence→Characterization/Recovery
	​


The central experimental hypothesis is not that a crystal supplies additional energy.

Rather, EVAPS-PC asks whether an appropriately selected dielectric or crystalline structure placed within or adjacent to an electrically generated plasma can measurably alter useful plasma characteristics—including charge distribution, electron density, ionization fraction, electric-field distribution, stability, temperature, or downstream electromagnetic coupling.

The first POC therefore treats the crystal chamber as a passive or externally biased plasma-conditioning element whose effects must be established experimentally against an otherwise identical control channel.

13. POC Development Program

A sensible progression would be:

POC-E1 — Electrical plasma baseline. Establish a stable plasma source and characterize input power, flow, temperature and plasma properties.
POC-E2 — Passive dielectric test. Compare an empty channel against several characterized dielectric inserts.
POC-E3 — Crystal geometry experiment. Test the proposed crystal chamber while keeping flow geometry, source power and gas conditions controlled.
POC-E4 — Active polarization. Add independently powered electrodes/field structures and characterize whether dielectric geometry changes the response.
POC-E5 — Split/reconverge experiment. Divide the plasma into multiple paths, condition them separately and determine whether differences persist after recombination.
POC-E6 — Downstream coupling. Send conditioned and unconditioned plasma through the same electromagnetic extraction apparatus and compare the resulting coupling.
POC-E7 — Integrated EVAPS-PC. Operate generator → splitter → crystal chamber → reconvergence → extraction while instrumenting every energy boundary.

The progression deliberately makes every stage falsifiable.

15. Failure Criteria

EVAPS-PC should be considered unsuccessful in its proposed role if the experiment finds that the crystal/dielectric stage merely heats the gas, destabilizes the discharge, produces no measurable difference from an inert insert, causes unacceptable erosion, or requires more conditioning power than any useful downstream improvement could justify.

That's not wasted work.

A negative result eliminates an entire branch of the architecture cheaply, before building an integrated powerplant.

16. Revised EVAPS Definition

EVAPS-PC is an electrically powered plasma research architecture that divides a controlled plasma stream, passes the resulting streams through characterized dielectric/crystalline field-conditioning structures, and reconverges them to determine whether the conditioning process produces measurable and energetically useful changes in downstream plasma behavior.

Its experimental doctrine becomes:

Generate→Separate→Polarize/Condition→Re-converge→Measure→Recover
	​


That gives you a particularly strong first POC because the crystal chamber itself can be validated or falsified without having to prove the entire vehicle architecture simultaneously.

And if it works, then PAIR, PCRAS, PEVT-LKR, and the storage/recovery architecture become downstream engineering problems rather than assumptions piled on assumptions. The VAPS-KERS work could ultimately provide transient storage downstream, but it remains a separate energy-storage subsystem rather than part of the plasma-generation claim.
