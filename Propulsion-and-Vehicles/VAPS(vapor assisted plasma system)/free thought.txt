What I'd project for development

I'd now establish three performance milestones rather than advertise one theoretical MPG number.

VAPS-RIC Alpha: target 25–30% fuel-to-DC. The purpose is proving stable combustion, rotor operation, induction generation, thermal isolation and net-positive plasma assistance. That's approximately 30–36 mpg equivalent in our hypothetical vehicle.

VAPS-RIC Beta: target 30–40% fuel-to-DC through optimized rotor geometry, recuperation and reduced parasitic losses. That's roughly 36–48 mpg equivalent.

Mature VAPS-RIC: target 40–50% fuel-to-DC. That corresponds to approximately 48–60 mpg equivalent before considering vehicle-level benefits.

I would treat 50%+ as an aspirational research target, not something the white paper should promise.
